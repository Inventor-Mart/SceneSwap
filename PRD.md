## 🧭 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PRODUCT VISION

**SceneSwap** is an interactive party entertainment and collaborative filmmaking engine designed to turn any social gathering, family game night, or friend hangout into a live movie set. 

By eliminating the rigid rules, passive bystander waiting, and steep learning curves of traditional board games, SceneSwap instantly casts real participants into personalized, high-energy screenplays. Spanning vibrant and culturally authentic genres—such as Nollywood Drama, Sci-Fi Survival, Cyberpunk Heist, Murder Mystery, Romantic Comedy, and Yoruba Royal Epics—each story dynamically evolves through live group choice voting, expressive teleprompter pacing, photorealistic scene backdrops, and interactive sound effects.

### Mission Statement
To make collaborative storytelling and spontaneous acting effortless, hilarious, and deeply personal for every social circle and content creator worldwide.

## 🎯 2. PROBLEM STATEMENT & VALUE PROPOSITION

### 2.1 The Core Problems
* **Party Game Fatigue & Predictability:** Traditional board and card games become stale after repeated play and cannot adapt to the unique inside jokes of a friend group.
* **Performance Anxiety in Improv:** People love witty banter and comedic drama, but feel paralyzed when asked to improvise without a script or structure.
* **Passive Bystander Syndrome:** Many group party games leave most participants sitting idle while only one or two people play.
* **Social Video Content Creation Bottlenecks:** Short-form video creators spend excessive time writing sketches, formatting lines, and directing friends for TikTok and Instagram Reels.

### 2.2 The SceneSwap Solution
* **Personalized Casting:** Every friend is cast as a distinct character with their exact name, unique archetype, dramatic motivation, and a secret agenda.
* **Live Teleprompter Pacing:** Spoken dialogue is presented in an intuitive, auto-advancing format complete with emotional acting delivery cues in brackets.
* **Branching 3-Act & 5-Act Narratives:** The room votes on dramatic crossroads that fundamentally alter the story's trajectory.
* **Cinematic Immersion:** Full-frame photorealistic backgrounds and reactive sound effects transport players directly into the universe of their scene.

## 🚀 4. CORE PRODUCT EXPERIENCES & USER FLOW

### 4.1 Frictionless Welcome
A clean, elegant welcome interface featuring a single call-to-action to proceed directly into the cast studio without unnecessary introductory clutter or setup obstacles.

### 4.2 Cast Studio & Avatar Customization
* **Cast Capacity:** Accommodates 2 to 5 live party players per production.
* **Custom Face Avatars:** Allows players to snap or upload a selfie directly from their device, or automatically use authentic genre-based portraits.
* **Player Presets:** Quick-roster loaders for recurring groups (e.g., "Lagos Squad", "London Crew", "Office Drama").
* **Visual Color Coding:** Assigns each participant a dedicated color identity that highlights their dialogue and turn order across the teleprompter.

### 4.3 Curated Genres & Mood Styling
SceneSwap provides eight rich, cinematic genres:
1. **Nollywood Drama & High Society:** Extravagant family empires, contested wills, courtroom showdowns, and fierce matriarch power struggles.
2. **Sci-Fi Survival & Orbital Hazard:** Zero-gravity airlock breaches, mysterious synthetic mists, rogue space station AI, and spacewalk gambits.
3. **Cyberpunk Heist & Neon Syndicate:** Midnight rain, encrypted neural chips, corporate hunter drones, and black-market data brokers.
4. **Murder Mystery & Manor Intrigue:** Secret poisonings, hidden tape recordings, vintage drawing rooms, and dramatic parlor accusations.
5. **Zombie Apocalypse & Road Survival:** Barricaded safehouses, frantic supply runs, bite-mark checks, and high-speed highway escapes.
6. **Romantic Comedy & Wedding Chaos:** Fake relationships, dramatic rehearsal dinner speeches, runaway partners, and airport chases.
7. **Yoruba Royal Epic & Throne War:** Ancient imperial coronations, palace intrigue, warrior oaths, and sacred talking drum declarations.
8. **Paranormal Horror & Ghost Manor:** Flickering lamps, haunted family artifacts, encrypted EVP voices, and stormy midnight rituals.

### 4.4 Wildcard Hook & Inside Joke Injector
Hosts can input custom prompt seeds—such as friend nicknames, workplace rumors, or wedding roasts—or roll situational dice to introduce wild twists into the narrative.

### 4.5 Extended Act Generation & Theatrical Pacing
Each act is a full, extended scene featuring **14 to 20 spoken dialogue lines**, guaranteeing:
* Every cast member speaks at least 3 to 4 times per act.
* Multi-beat back-and-forth dialogue exchanges rather than short one-liners.
* Rising dramatic tension leading to a cliffhanger voting crossroads.

### 4.6 Dual Presentation Modes
* **Mobile Studio Script / TikTok Live View:** Optimized 9:16 vertical view featuring an unobstructed realistic scene backdrop, live active actor spotlight, bracketed acting directions, and teleprompter controls.
* **Cinematic Stage View:** Wide landscape layout designed for television mirroring or living room monitors.

### 4.7 Photorealistic Scene Imagery
Every act generates a fresh, photorealistic 9:16 cinematic backdrop capturing realistic lighting, depth of field, and environmental mood tailored to the exact location.

### 4.8 Interactive Soundboard & Audio FX Deck
One-touch acting sound effects integrated directly into the live teleprompter:
* **Vine Boom** (dramatic sub-bass shock)
* **Suspense Sting** (orchestral tension swell)
* **Record Scratch** (comedic halt)
* **Comedic Boing** (playful sound effect)
* **Thunder Crash** (atmospheric drama)
* **Crowd Applause** (victory cheer)

### 4.9 Choice Matrix & Group Voting
At the conclusion of each act, the ensemble votes on three distinct plot choices (Option A, Option B, Option C), each complete with consequence teasers that steer the following chapter.

### 4.10 Grand Finale & Script Export
Act 3 (or Act 5 in Epic Mode) concludes with a dramatic story resolution, an epilogue summary, and one-click script export for sharing to social media or messaging apps.
